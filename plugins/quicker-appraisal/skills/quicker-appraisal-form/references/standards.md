# Israeli appraisal standards - what each one asks the report (and therefore the form) to hold

Knowledge base for designing appraisal forms. Researched October 2026 from the primary sources
(gov.il - מועצת שמאי המקרקעין / הוועדה לתקינה שמאית, the ethics regulations, Bank of Israel
directives). Standards are revised often (nine were renewed in 2025-2026): when a user's question turns
on an exact clause, say which version you rely on and suggest checking gov.il. Items marked
**[unverified]** could not be confirmed from the primary text - never present them as certain.

## 1. How binding is what

- **Mandatory by law - תקנות שמאי מקרקעין (אתיקה מקצועית), התשכ"ו-1966.** The Addendum (per reg.
  1(16)) lists the minimum every "דו"ח שומה" must hold; a report without them is "התנהגות שאינה הולמת".
  Reg. 1(1) also makes service "שלא לפי הכללים, התקנים והנוהלים המקובלים במקצוע" improper conduct.
- **The standards (הוועדה לתקינה שמאית)** are, by the committee's own procedure (approved 11.2.2024),
  "נורמת עבודה מומלצת" - except where legislation gives a standard binding force. Example: **standard
  22** is binding through consumer-protection regulations (2016). In practice appraisers treat the
  standards as the professional norm, and deviating has disciplinary weight (reg. 1(1)).
- **Deviation**: every standard allows deviating if the report states what and why - except **standard
  19 §4.9-4.18 (collateral), where no deviation is allowed**; if a material item is missing, no
  collateral appraisal may be issued. Standard 8.1 puts deviations in "עקרונות, גורמים ושיקולים".
  -> A form for standard-governed work benefits from a "סטייה מהתקינה + נימוק" field.
- **Which version applies** is decided by the **signing date** of the appraisal ("שומות שייחתמו
  מיום X"), except 17.1 (by the valuation date). Today the important switch is **standard 9: 9.0 until
  31.12.2026, 9.1 from 1.1.2027** (apartment area definition changes - see §5).
- **Order of sections is part of the standard (1.1 §7):** headings -> purpose/orderer -> valuation date
  -> visit -> identification -> description (5.1) -> planning (6.1) -> rights (7.1) -> history ->
  experts -> principles (8.1) -> calculation -> value -> signature -> declarations. Newer standards
  insist on separation: a fact must not appear for the first time in the principles section; physical
  details do not belong in the rights section. Forms that mirror this order make reports that mirror it.
- **Source documentation is now required** (6.1, 7.1): keep query results, documents and extracts in
  the appraisal's computer folder; information given orally is recorded with content, giver's name,
  role and date. Forms often carry a "מקור הנתון" next to key facts (Quicker's `textSod` type exists for
  this).

## 3. What every report must hold

**Ethics regulations, Addendum - the legal minimum of every report:**
1. זיהוי הנכס (מיקום, מהות, שטח, גוש, חלקה או מגרש לפי תב"ע, מען)
2. תיאור הנכס והסביבה
3. הזכויות המוערכות (בעלות, חכירה, דיירות מוגנת...)
4. המצב התכנוני (ייעוד, תכניות חלות)
5. מטרת השומה
6. הודעת השמאי על ביקור בנכס - מי ביקר ומתי
7. המועד הקובע
8. העקרונות שלפיהם נערכה השומה
9. אפשרויות השימוש בנכס, לרבות שינוי שימוש ותוספות בנייה אפשריות
10. נעזר במומחה: זהותו, מומחיותו והעניין שבו חיווה דעה
11. מצב הרישוי בנכס (added תשפ"ב)
12. תחשיבים (added תשפ"ב)
13. שכר לפי תוצאה (reg. 4) - יצוין

Other ethics rules that become form fields: a court opinion requires a **personal visit by the signing
appraiser** (reg. 1(3)(א)); otherwise the visit may be by the signer, a partner, another appraiser, an
intern in the office, or an employee who finished internship (1(3)(ב)) -> "תפקיד המבקר" is a closed list.
Conflict of interest: appraising a property the appraiser brokered or handled as a lawyer within **3
years** (1(11)). Contingent fee (reg. 4) only in listed proceedings (שמאי מכריע/מייעץ, ועדת ערר,
ועדת השגות, השגה ברמ"י) and capped - needs its own declaration.

**Standard 1.1 (from 1.4.2025)** - two classes, written in the title:
- "**שומת מקרקעין מלאה**" - meets the ethics regulations.
- "**שומת מקרקעין מקיפה**" - full + more (court / high disclosure). For court the title is
  "**חוות דעת של מומחה**" with the fixed expert declaration (appendix ב': name, workplace,
  "אני נותן חוות דעתי זו במקום עדות בבית המשפט...", education, experience).
- There is **no "שומה מקוצרת" class** in the standards; a short bank format is bank practice (§8).
- The old "שומה מבוססת הנחה היפותטית" class is gone - replaced by a disclosure duty (below).

| item | מלאה | מקיפה |
|---|---|---|
| type + date in title, נמען, סימוכין, "הנדון" (מהות + גוש/חלקה + כתובת) | + | + |
| מזמין: שם, תפקיד, זיקה לנכס | + | + |
| מטרת השומה; הזכויות הנשומות (בעלות/חכירה/שכירות...) | + | + |
| המועד הקובע - fixed wording "המועד הקובע לשומה הוא ..." | + | + |

Declarations (1.1 §6.14): no personal interest ("אני מצהיר כי אין לי כל עניין אישי בנכס נשוא השומה,
בבעלי הזכויות בו או במזמין השומה"); prepared per the ethics regulations and the committee's standards;
contingent-fee declaration when relevant.

**Value based on an assumption (1.1 §8)** - when the orderer asks to assume a physical/planning state
that doesn't exist (e.g. built per permit, a plan in preparation approved), or a fact can't be checked:
title says "מבוססת הנחה", the assumption is shown **in a box** in the description/planning section, and
the value section lists the assumptions and who asked for them. Not an "assumption": the appraiser's
own working assumptions, values a public authority imposes by its procedure (היטל השבחה, רמ"י), rights
valued that don't exist in practice. -> forms that serve such work need "הנחות שאינן תואמות את
המציאות" + "מי ביקש".

## 4. Section standards

**4.2 זיהוי הנכס (from 1.8.2025), in this order:** גוש (unsettled land: "גוש שומא" or ספר/דף +
"(מקרקעין לא מוסדרים)"; unregistered: "מקרקעין לא רשומים") / חלקה (incl. 3D parcel) / תת-חלקה (only
in a registered condominium) / מגרש + the plan that created it / מהות הנכס ("דירה בת 3 חדרים בבניין
מגורים רווי") / כתובת (street+number, neighbourhood, town) / מיקום במבנה / קומה וכיוון (counting:
ground, gallery, 1, 2...; ground/pillars and gallery are not floors) / מספר יחידה if seen at the visit.
Aids: ITM coordinates from Govmap (+link), map/תשריט/תצ"א with source, project name/developer/חברה
משכנת. Physical identification at the visit: whole parcel via Govmap/GPS; part of a parcel via
contract/sharing-agreement plan; unit in a registered condominium via תשריט בית משותף, otherwise via
contract/agreement/permit; if impossible - say so and why.

**5.1 תיאור הנכס והסביבה (from 1.8.2025)** - a checklist: opening sentence (nature + general location);
position in the building (floor, front/back, façade directions); **built area + its source**; parts and
internal division; finish (height, flooring, cladding, windows, A/C, electricity, upkeep); **הצמדות and
"שטחים משויכים"** (exclusive use without a property right, e.g. a roof reachable only from the flat);
the building (floors, units, uses, common property, lifts); year built; land area; **use at the
valuation date/visit and who uses it (owner/tenant/other)**; land shape/topography; neighbourhood
(character, building years, parking, noise, population); ± **distance to mass transit (רכבת קלה,
רכבת, מטרו)** in effective walking distance (source: משרד התחבורה "חצב" or Govmap). Commercial: main
and secondary frontage widths and depth. Comprehensive report: photo + map/תצ"א with source (and date).

**6.1 המצב התכנוני (signed from 1.1.2026)** - mandatory collection order: the local committee's site
("דף מידע תכנוני" per §119א where available) -> מנהל התכנון -> other sites / ask the committee ->
infrastructure bodies (e.g. נת"י) as needed; save query results; record oral info (content, name,
role, date); if a body didn't answer, say so and the assumptions made. Detail level: low for built
property / value in use; **high for speculative land, היטל השבחה, 197 compensation, expropriation**.
Per plan: type, number, name, **status + status date with publication reference** (statuses: תקפה /
אושרה וטרם פורסמה / מופקדת / הוחלט להפקיד / הוכרז לפי סעיף 77), designation and uses, building
rights and provisions, conditions for a permit, §78 instructions, appendices, the planning body's
interpretation. Building file: per permit/application - number, date or status, nature (temporary
permit: period), **area table per floor: existing and requested, by use (עיקרי/שירות)**, plan
excerpts, missing information + assumptions. Other: טופס 4/5, offences, inspection reports, demolition
orders, dangerous structure, preservation, **business licence** where value depends on it.
**חריגות בנייה (§10) are mandatory to state** incl. use contrary to the permit; assessed from a
willing buyer's view (severity, spare rights allowing legalisation, age, construction type).

**7.1 הזכויות בנכס (signed from 1.9.2026)** - registration status is mandatory in every appraisal.
Order: register -> rights confirmations -> contracts. **נסח** from the טאבו site, issued **up to 3
months** before (when the valuation date = report date); opening line: register type (זכויות / בתים
משותפים / שטרות) + extract date. Items: registered property type; parcel area; sub-parcel area;
owners and shares (may summarise, stating the appraised share); lessees + lease terms if shown;
**share in common property as a fraction**; הצמדות; **תקנון מצוי/מוסכם** (agreed bylaws: clauses that
affect value, e.g. attached building rights); relevant notes (warning notes, mortgages, easements go
here); for unregistered reparcellation - the original parcels. Do not paste the extract as an image -
attach it. Then: rights confirmation (רמ"י / חברה משכנת / אגודה - type and date; say if missing);
unregistered transfers (sale/inheritance/gift: source, nature, date, parties); leases (reference, date,
parties, **term and options**, purpose, area, **rent**); occupants and claimants (protected tenant,
trespasser, share held); management company and fees. Fixed wordings for unregistered ("הנכס נשוא
השומה טרם נרשם בפנקסי המקרקעין") and unsettled land. Detail: low for fair rent; high for sale and
collateral.

**8.1 עקרונות, גורמים ושיקולים (signed from 1.10.2026)** - after the three factual sections, before
the calculation, numbered sub-sections: purpose (may repeat); **approaches used and why - and why a
customary approach was not used (mandatory)**; property characteristics behind the adjustment factors;
**explanation of material values** (capitalisation rate, material factors, external sources such as a
cost calculator, an expert, Delphi); general factors (policy, case law, supply/demand, expectations,
potential, taxes); general notes: **deviations + reasons, working assumptions that don't match
reality, foreign/historic currency, VAT, reservations (הסתייגויות), movables**. Not allowed: copying
textbook definitions of the approaches; presenting facts for the first time.

## 5. Areas

**9.0 (signed until 31.12.2026):** שטח דירה = interior + exterior walls + half of shared walls; includes
covered balconies, ממ"ד, storerooms and covered service areas when attached, entered from the flat
only. Open balconies, yards, roof, storeroom, parking measured separately. Report the source ("עפ"י
מדידה פיזית" / "עפ"י מדידה גראפית מתשריט" with 3 dimensions checked / a reliable written source) and
"שטח הדירה תואם תקן זה".

**9.1 (signed from 1.1.2027)** - applies to owned flats in apartment buildings (2+ floors, 4+ units)
and ground-attached houses: outer line of exterior walls **at full thickness**; centre of a wall shared
with another flat; facing common area or a shaft - **10 cm inside the wall from the flat's face**.
Included only if enclosed, continuous, with direct permanent access from the flat and no free access to
others. ממ"ד and a closed covered service balcony - included. **Sun balconies and roofs (covered or
not) are NOT in the flat area** - reported separately as attachments (may split covered/uncovered).
Attic counts only with fixed built stairs, the part ≥ 180 cm high. Storeroom inside only if attached
with access from the flat only, otherwise separate. Parking per marking. Report in the description
section, multi-level flats per level, **source next to each figure**. Source hierarchy: surveyor's map
per the standard / the specification under חוק המכר (contracts from 27.5.2008) / **registered area
only in a condominium registered from 1.1.2020** (older registered area is a control only). Graphic
measurement: surveyor map > condominium plan > permit plan, at least 3 long dimensions verified.
-> In 2027 a form needs: שטח דירה (9.1), balconies separately (covered/uncovered), attachments
separately, the source of each area, and probably keep "שטח רשום" as a control figure.

**10.0 non-residential:** interior area incl. exterior wall up to 25 cm and half shared walls
(ממ"ד/ממ"ק included but stated separately; covered balconies separately); floor common areas; building
common areas; basement (main areas separately; service/parking + wall up to 20 cm); parking = number of
standard spaces per the permit. Source: the permit, otherwise condominium plan or measurement - **never
ארנונה**. Measured: method, date, surveyor, "תואם תקן". Minimum detail by type: retail - interior +
attachments; offices / industry - interior + floor common + building common + attachments; car parks -
interior + floor common + attachments. Areas without a permit separately. Ratio interior / (interior +
floor common).

"שטח אקוויוולנטי", "שטח רשום", "שטח עיקרי/שירות" and "ערך כינון" are **not defined** in the dictionary
or in 9.x - use them as the office does, but don't attribute a definition to a standard.

## 6. Valuation approaches

**2.1 built property (from 1.7.2025)** - flats, offices, industry, retail (not: vacant land or
highest-and-best-use = demolition -> 3.1; income-producing residential like assisted living is not
bound). Comparison: **at least 3 comparables, within 2 years before or after the valuation date,
sum of absolute adjustments ≤ 35%**; the property's own sale is the best comparable. Low-quality data
(offers - adjust for negotiation; other appraisals - quasi-judicial decisions weigh more) when fewer
than 3. Recommended comparables table: date + property details; price + source; price per unit +
adjustment factors; adjusted price. Source reliability: public tender > contract > tax-authority report
> informal. Income: perpetual capitalisation of **gross fair rent** at an **overall cap rate**
(operating costs and vacancy are inside the rate, not separate items); fair rent from **≥3 rents**;
report rents table, fair rent, cap rate (and survey + who ran it), economic advantage/disadvantage of
an existing lease. Cost: land + construction + entrepreneurship (old property: depreciation),
business property **without VAT**, regional grants.

**3.1 land (from 1.9.2026)** - comparison: ≥3 transactions, close to the valuation date (older than 2
years = low quality), adjustments ≤ 35% (time adjusted separately); unit of benefit מ"ר / יחידה /
**מ"ר מבונה אקוויוולנטי**. Residual (חילוץ): built value -> minus VAT (full rate, even when not new;
private-buyers market: no VAT deduction, lower profit) -> minus developer profit -> minus construction
costs ex-VAT -> grants -> purchase tax and costs. **Validity: land component < 20% of built value
(ex-VAT) = low, > 20% = medium.** Report: comparables for built value, VAT rate, profit rate,
construction costs + source, depreciation, grants, other costs, residual land value. Agricultural:
private land and רמ"י leasehold are different property types; expectation value may be discounted when
planning is advanced (e.g. after a §77 notice) without making it "assumption-based"; orchards and
greenhouses by net income or cost. Demolition-HBU: land value + temporary income + saved purchase tax,
levies and connections. Not covered: public uses, petrol stations, hotels, assisted living,
מחיר למשתכן/מחיר מטרה/long-term rental land.

**11.0א VAT:** private-buyers market (flats, houses, a plot for one house, parking/extension rights) -
value includes VAT and the principles section must say the market is mainly private buyers not liable
for VAT; **for רמ"י also state a theoretical value without VAT**. Business/public property (offices,
retail, industry, petrol, hotels, public buildings, land for them, **land for apartment-building
construction**) - without VAT, written "לא כולל מע"מ" next to the value. Mixed market: generally
without VAT by criteria. Exempt sales (Eilat, key money, residential rent up to 25 years): fixed
wording. -> a "מע"מ: כולל / לא כולל / פטור" field is useful on every form.

## 7. Purpose-specific standards

**19.0 collateral for credit (bank, insurer, other lender, bond issues) - NOT residential mortgages.**
"שומת מקרקעין מלאה", usable by any lender. Valuation date ≤ 3 months before the report (unless a
historic date was asked); visit ≤ 3 months, who presented the property and their relation, **the
visitor's title** (appraiser/intern); all parts (single-use like a hotel excepted) - **a part not
inspected is excluded from the collateral value and highlighted**. Photos: façade, interior, business
sign. Planning: valid non-conforming uses and those expired in the last **7 years**; **unused rights
table** (plans, permit incl. relaxations, balance per floor, main/service); interpretation doubts;
**soil contamination** (survey conditions, opinions, uses the Environment Ministry defines as suspect).
Rights: **extract ≤ 2 months, only from the official source**; רמ"י confirmation ≤ 3 months (document
the request; no answer in 21 days -> say so and appraise on what exists); lease terms (term, purpose,
capacity, rent); tenants table + occupancy, **rent payments verified with the owner's CPA for the last
quarter (not for residential)**, management fees, electricity income, sharing agreements, protected
tenants, unrealised expropriations, §126 warning notes, third-party agreements. History: this
appraiser's appraisals and transactions within 3 years. Approaches: high-validity ones; income
property **comparison and income**; gap > 20% between approaches must be explained. **Two values:
שווי שוק and "ערך למטרת בטוחה"** (approved plans only, no illegal construction, no assumptions in the
borrower's favour); planning potential shown separately; reservations next to the value. **Mandatory
(4.15): forced-sale discount (%), taxes on realisation - מס שבח without personal exemptions, היטל
השבחה without exemptions, payments to the lessor, other charges.** **4.16 reinstatement value (ערך
כינון): replacement cost, without VAT, without entrepreneurship, ignoring physical depreciation.**
Annexes: extract, rights confirmation, photos, map, allocation table, lease. Bonds: also 17.1.

**17.1 securities / accounting (IFRS, IAS 40):** title "שומת מקרקעין מקיפה"; no assuming verifiable
facts; engagement: company, who ordered and role, date, circumstances (prospectus, reports, interested
party deal), **accounting classification (investment property / PP&E)**, contingent fee /
indemnity / dependence (or a statement there is none); appraiser's name, licence, education,
experience; **consent to publication**; visit; identification incl. land area, built area, rights;
rights: extract/confirmation ≤ 3 months, **tenants table by use and sector, a tenant over 20% of area
or income disclosed (unnamed)**, renewals in the last year, **occupancy**, protected tenants,
management by a related company -> CPA-opined report; history: **purchase in the last 5 years (date,
price)**, transactions and appraisals incl. by other appraisers in 3 years, **gap > 20% highlighted**;
residual is not a stand-alone approach for vacant land (except construction/accounting); value in use
only per IAS 36; risks and opportunities; actual vs highest-and-best use; calculation **in the body,
not an annex**; gap > 20% between approaches explained; **sensitivity analysis (for income - on the
cap rate)**; experts with consent and dependence statement; value in ₪, reservations, **book value**,
VAT per 11.0א; expected taxes (היטל השבחה, רמ"י fees, third-party claims); declarations incl. relation
to owners/orderer. **[unverified]** whether the 3-month visit limit counts from the valuation date or the
report date.

**20.0 inspection report without a value:** must say it is not a valuation and not legal advice;
valuation date = report date; visit ≤ 3 months; extract ≤ 2 months; רמ"י confirmation ≤ 3 months;
identification (4), description (5), planning (6) incl. deviations and business licence, rights (7);
taxes listed **without amounts**; declarations; name, licence number, signature; which optional items
(division, finish, condition) were ordered.

**14.0 review:** three kinds - "סקירה עיונית", "סקירת שטח" (+ visit), "סקירה מלאה" (= a full appraisal
+ a response chapter). Always: kind and date in the title; orderer (name, role, relation to the review
and the property); **the reviewed appraisal: appraiser + licence, date + reference, property, purpose,
valuation date, intended user**; checks of the physical/planning/legal data and of methods and
calculation per the standards in force at the reviewed date; conclusions; declarations - in desk/field
reviews "בסקירה זו לא אומתו הנתונים שהוצגו בשומה הנסקרת". The reviewer may state a different value with
a calculation, stressing the data was not verified.

**21.1 urban renewal - פינוי-בינוי only (תמ"א 38 is not covered):** three parts - (א) plan
feasibility = "שומת מקרקעין מבוססת הנחה"; (ב) consideration for typical flats (§2א of the law); (ג) a
specific unit (reg. 19 of 2012). Valuation date close to the deposit discussion, **≤ 6 months before the
deposit decision**; visit ≤ 3 months, revisit after 2 years or a material change. Existing state:
units and areas per permits (otherwise measurement, extract, plan, ארנונה); rights built with/without
permit and balances; the agreement with the developer and number of signatories. Calculation (§5.14):
existing state and programme; income ex-VAT; construction, indirect, demolition, special costs;
**tenants' costs (rent, legal, moving, VAT)**; maintenance (gap × 0.8); taxes (השבחה, רכישה, שבח,
מע"מ); finance (5-10 years); land payments; **developer profit vs the government appraiser's table**
(versions 2022 and 2025). Sensitivity: profit vs consideration at +0 / +12 / +25 מ"ר. An official Excel
template accompanies the standard. Part ג criteria: area, attachments, location, **material renovation
in the last 7 years**, extra rights, accessibility adaptations. Minimum developer profit (government
appraiser, update 30.12.2025): Tel Aviv district 16% (15-17%), Centre 16%, Jerusalem 16%, Haifa / North /
South 17% **[secondary source - verify current table]**.

**22.0 land marketed to the public on expectations (binding via consumer regulations 2016):** title
"שומה מבוססת הנחה"; fixed valuation-date (= preparation) and visit wordings; jurisdiction and planning
area; legal state **without lenient interpretation**, incl. the seller's purchase agreement. **Two
values: מצב א'** per approved plans (no comparables that embed expectations) and **מצב ב'**
hypothetical: the programme and who gave it, **likelihood** (if remote - no value for מצב ב'; remote by
presumption when contrary to a national/district plan designating a reserve, park, beach or
infrastructure), **time with milestones**, costs (planning, development, levies, trespassers, מושע).
Mandatory clarification: "חוות הדעת אינה מיועדת לקביעת שווי השוק... ולא ניתן לעשות שמוש בחוות הדעת
בכל ערכאה משפטית ו/או בוועדות ערר". The seller must hand the buyer the appraisal ≥ 7 days before
contracting, with a summary of: likelihood, current designation, current value, expected time,
total costs and the buyer's expected share.

**15.0 allocation and balancing tables (איחוד וחלוקה)** and **16.0 decisive appraisal** - only the
titles and dates were verified (16.0: also applies to court-appointed deciding appraisals; the decisive
appraiser may ask a party to present its own appraisal). Procedure before a decisive appraiser
(regulations 2008): documents within 21 days (extract, the committee's decision/appraisal, the
applicant's appraisal), answer 30 days, hearing 45 days, decision 60 days after the last hearing.

## 8. Purposes with no dedicated standard

| purpose | what applies |
|---|---|
| **Residential mortgage (bank)** | Standard 19 explicitly excludes it. Ethics regs + 1.1 + **Bank of Israel directive 451** §10: independent of the bank's information, **physical visit and a sketch (תרשים) of the property**, "detailed description" and a declaration of the visit date; §9(ה): another bank's appraisal may be reused if the appraiser is on the bank's list and **90 days** have not passed; the borrower gets a copy. Directive 329: LTV caps (75% single flat, 70% replacement, 50% investment). The short bank format is each bank's own (Leumi: "רגילה" / "מורחבת עפ"י תקן 19"; Mizrahi-Tefahot: the appraiser records **ערך כינון**). No unified bank format was found. |
| **Construction finance (דו"ח אפס, דוחות פיקוח)** | No standard and no Bank of Israel content rule (directive 326 governs the closed-finance mechanism, not the report). Interpretation: "as completed" value is an assumption -> 1.1 §8; if used as collateral, 19 may apply. Build the form from the lending bank's template. |
| **היטל השבחה** | No standard yet (on the 2026-2027 work plans). 6.1 high detail; decisive-appraisal procedure; reg. 4 contingent fee + declaration. Third Addendum to the planning law: levy = half the betterment; appraised as of the plan's effective date (relaxation / non-conforming use - the approval date), "as if sold in a free market"; successive plans without levy in between -> before the first vs after the last; exemptions §19 (e.g. 19(ג)(1) residential up to 140 מ"ר). |
| **Planning damage §197** | No standard. 6.1 high detail; contingent fee allowed. Claim within 3 years of the plan's effect; valuation date = plan's effective date. |
| **Expropriation** | No standard (planned). 6.1 high; contingent fee allowed (Land Ordinance §9א, planning law §190). |
| **Partition / court** | Court-appointed -> "שומה מקיפה" / "חוות דעת של מומחה" (1.1 appendix ב') + personal visit by the signer. Court administration guidelines: case details, §24 Evidence Ordinance declaration, conflict-of-interest form, the questions as worded, documents reviewed, method, reply to the parties' opinions. Divorce: value at the separation date ("מועד הקרע"). |
| **רמ"י / leasehold** | 7.1 (rights confirmation), 19 / 17.1 (lease terms, payments to the lessor), 11.0א (theoretical value without VAT), 1.1 §8 (a רמ"י appraisal under its procedures is not "assumption-based"); the chief government appraiser's guidelines are a separate layer. |
| **Fair rent** | Dictionary definition; 2.1 §5.3 (≥3 rents); 7.1 low detail. |
| **Retrospective** | No standard. 1.1 §6.3 (events after the valuation date described but not priced; sales of the property after the date may serve as comparables), §6.12 (historic currency + ₪), 2.1 (comparables up to 2 years after the date). 7.1's 3-month extract rule applies only when the valuation date is the report date. |
| **"שומה מקוצרת"** | Not a standard class. Alternatives that are regulated: 20.0 (inspection without value), 14.0 (desk review). |
